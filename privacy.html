Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Information we collect

  • TikTok account information: after you authorize, we receive the open_id, avatar and display name returned by TikTok, plus the access token and refresh token needed to publish videos.
  • Content you upload: video files, captions and hashtags you submit, along with your publishing task records and statuses.
  • Operational logs: necessary API call logs kept to keep the Service running (never containing passwords).

2. How we use this information

This information is used only to: complete the authorization you initiate, publish the videos you upload to the TikTok accounts you authorized, show you publishing task status, and troubleshoot failures. We never use your content to train models and never sell it to third parties.

3. Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties except:

  • where required to publish — your video and caption must be submitted to TikTok for the accounts you authorized;
  • technical providers strictly necessary to operate the Service (such as server hosting), who process data only on our instructions;
  • where required by law or to protect legitimate rights.

4. Storage and retention

Data is stored on the servers that run the Service and is cleared on a reasonable schedule after you revoke authorization or delete your account. Access tokens are stored encrypted in our server database and are never written to logs or displayed.

5. Your rights

At any time you may:

  • revoke the Service's authorization in TikTok under "Settings and privacy — Security and permissions";
  • request deletion of your account data and task history;
  • request an export of the content you uploaded.

6. Security measures

We protect data with transport encryption (HTTPS), server access controls, and the principle of least privilege. No internet transmission is perfectly secure, so please also safeguard your own account credentials.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for adult operators. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us using the contact email registered on the TikTok for Developers portal.
