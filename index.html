What MultiPost Studio does

Everything a small team needs to run several TikTok accounts without logging in and out of devices all day.

👥

Multi-account management

Connect multiple TikTok accounts in one dashboard. Each account is authorized individually through TikTok's official Login Kit.

🎬

Upload once, publish anywhere

Upload a local video file, add caption and hashtags, then select any combination of connected accounts as publish targets.

⏰

Minute-level scheduling

Publish immediately or schedule to a specific date and time — accurate to the minute. A built-in queue fires each task automatically.

🔀

Automatic staggered posting

When publishing to several accounts, tasks are automatically spaced out to respect TikTok's rate limits and keep accounts safe.

📊

Publish status tracking

Every task shows its live status — queued, uploading, published, or failed — so you always know what went out and when.

🔗

Product anchoring

Optionally attach a TikTok Shop product link to a video so viewers can go straight from content to product page.

How it works

Three steps from sign-in to published video.

1

Connect your TikTok accounts

Click "Authorize account" and sign in with TikTok. You approve the requested scopes once per account and can revoke access at any time from your TikTok settings.

2

Upload a video & set the schedule

Pick a video file, write the caption with hashtags, choose target accounts, and set "Publish now" or an exact date and time.

3

We publish and you track

The scheduler uploads and posts each video through TikTok's official Content Posting API. Follow progress in the task list in real time.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how MultiPost Studio works.

Do I need to give MultiPost Studio my TikTok password?

No. Accounts are connected through TikTok's official OAuth login. You sign in on TikTok's own page and grant permission there — your password is never seen by MultiPost Studio.

Can I schedule posts to the minute?

Yes. When creating a task you can pick any future date and time with minute-level precision. The built-in scheduler publishes automatically at that moment.

How many accounts can I connect?

There is no hard limit in the product. Each account must be authorized individually, and publishing to many accounts in a short window is automatically staggered to respect TikTok's rate limits.

How do I stop MultiPost Studio from accessing my account?

Remove the account from the dashboard, or go to TikTok → Settings → Security & permissions ("Manage app permissions") and revoke access. Revocation takes effect immediately.
